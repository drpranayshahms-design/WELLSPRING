## 0. Read first: five things that change how you spend these credits

1. **Download everything as you generate.** The ElevenLabs history and any cloned voice stop being usable once the plan lapses. A file is only a "lifetime" asset once it is saved in Drive.
2. **Commercial rights.** Audio made on a paid plan generally keeps its commercial licence after you cancel. Before you start, take a screenshot of your current plan page and the ElevenLabs Terms / Music Terms and save it in `00_PLAN/` as proof of the licence. Music has its own terms. Check that Meta and YouTube use is covered for your plan tier.
3. **Credits may roll over.** On most paid plans, unused credits carry forward for a limited period if the subscription renews. Spend them now only if you will not renew. If renewing is cheap compared with Rs 3 lakh of unused value, renew one more month and work through this plan with less hurry.
4. **AI hospital or patient imagery is a compliance risk.** Under ASCI guidelines, NMC professional-conduct rules, and Meta's health-ad review, showing AI-generated "patients", "our lab" or "our babies" as real counts as misleading advertising. Use AI visuals only for abstract or conceptual B-roll and backgrounds. Real footage of your own lab and team will always build more trust and convert better.
5. **No claims in voice assets.** Keep "6000+ IVF successes", success percentages, "guaranteed", "best in Gujarat", and any reference to the baby's sex (PCPNDT) out of the reusable voice lines. One non-compliant stock line can get every ad that uses it rejected.
